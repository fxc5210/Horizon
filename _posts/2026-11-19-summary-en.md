### [Quad9 Withdraws From France Over DNS Blocking Mandates](https://techstackups.com/articles/quad9-leaves-france-dns-blocking/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Public DNS resolver Quad9 has withdrawn from France following legal and policy pressures regarding mandatory site-blocking for antipiracy enforcement. This case highlights ongoing technical and legal debates surrounding the use of infrastructure-level DNS resolvers to enforce intellectual property laws. Commenters debated whether the root issue stems from incompetent judicial interpretation or flawed legislative mandates.

hackernews · ritzaco · Nov 18, 13:18 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50545958)

**「Background」** Governments and rights holders increasingly utilize internet infrastructure providers, such as recursive DNS resolvers and internet service providers, to block access to domains accused of copyright infringement or piracy. This method bypasses traditional direct enforcement against content hosts or operators.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters argued that antipiracy measures targeting DNS infrastructure risk opening backdoors for broader government censorship. Others noted that legal streaming inconveniences compared to pirated alternatives continue to drive users toward unauthorized sources.
